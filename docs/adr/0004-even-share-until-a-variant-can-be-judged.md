# New subjects and bodies share Idle traffic evenly until they can be judged

The scorer picks Idle subjects and bodies by Thompson sampling, and retires a variant once it and the leader each have 120 sends and the leader is 90% likely to be better. With Thompson sampling alone, a poor variant is starved before it reaches 120 sends: in the simulation a subject of hidden quality 20 stalled at about 80 sends. It was never judged, so it was never retired, and the writer never got another turn. So until every variant in play has 120 sends, the variants share Idle traffic evenly; after that, Thompson sampling decides. Over six seeds this found a secretly much better subject 6 times out of 6, where pure Thompson sampling found it 0 times at 120 sends, and it roughly doubled the AI loop's Idle lift over the hand-written email (+0.49 against +0.23 points). The cost is that a proven leader gets half the Idle traffic for each newcomer's first 120 sends. A newcomer still starts from a cautious prior at the slot's average, so a few lucky clicks don't make it the leader at once. (Drafters and Trialists then got the Idle leader; since ADR 0005 they send their own first drafts.)
