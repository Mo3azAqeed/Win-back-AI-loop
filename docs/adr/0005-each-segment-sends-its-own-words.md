# Drafters and Trialists send their own words, and Idle's edge doesn't carry over by default

Drafter and Trialist segments are too small to test subjects and bodies, so the spec had them send whichever Idle subject and body led, and the simulation assumed that email was exactly as good for them as for Idle. Both halves were wrong: the words were Idle's ("you haven't started a store yet" sent to someone with a draft), and most of the AI loop's Trialist edge came from the assumption, not from anything it tested on Trialists. So Drafters and Trialists now send their own first drafts, and a carry-over setting (0 to 1) says how much of the Idle leader's edge, as a shift on the log-odds of reading and clicking, their emails get. The founder chose 0 as the default (2026-10-26): any AI loop win in those segments must be earned there, through timing now and offers once #8 lands. Real carry-over is expected to come from lessons feeding the writer (#9), not from an assumption. 0.5 and 1 remain as what-if settings.

With carry-over 0, the AI loop trails the hand-written email slightly in Drafter and Trialist (about −0.2 points, 10 seeds at 1,500 merchants): +2 days is already the best timing there, and testing the other two costs a little. At the placeholder rates, 13 weeks can't tell Drafter timings apart.

Each segment's choices now draw from their own random stream, so changing carry-over (or anything else in Drafter and Trialist) never changes Idle's results. Switching to these streams reshuffled the AI loop's luck once: single seeds moved, averages did not (Idle lift over 10 seeds is the same; carry-over 1 matches the old Drafter and Trialist results on average, +1.72 against +1.74 points and +2.58 against +2.43, but not seed for seed).

Drafters and Trialists get no prewritten variants of their own: they learn timing only (and Trialists offers, #8), so there is nothing to test their words against yet. Their own variants arrive with the real writer (#13).

Update (#36, 2026-10-26): Drafters and Trialists now borrow Idle's playbook. When an Idle test is won (not when a version is kept at the limit), the writer applies the same change to their emails in their own words, and that version replaces theirs with no test. Carry-over now applies to each borrowed part (its share of that Idle part's edge) rather than to the Idle champion as a whole, so at 0 nothing changes in their results; at 1, only proven changes help them. With the default settings Idle wins a test outright in about 1 run in 20 at 1,000 merchants and 10 in 20 at 1,500 (most changes are kept at the limit), so borrowing is rare at our volume.
