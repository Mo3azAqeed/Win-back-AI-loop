# Subjects and bodies learn from human clicks; timing and offers learn from everyone assigned

The research recommended rewarding every part of an email with the goal action. The prototype showed that at our volume (1,000–1,500 merchants over 13 weeks) that signal is too weak: most merchants who reach their goal would have done so anyway, so the scorer barely beat a hand-written email. Subjects and bodies therefore learn from human clicks (scanner clicks removed), which only the email can cause. Timing and offers still learn from the goal action, judged over a fixed number of days from the trigger for every merchant assigned that choice, including those who acted before the email went out; judging them by the window after the email credited early emails with merchants who would have acted anyway. Trialist offers are rewarded by revenue, so a 50% discount payment counts as half. Lessons need 99% certainty and 250 sends per variant, because a looser rule wrote false lessons when every email was secretly identical.
